AD 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS
1. U-TAPAO VFR LOCAL PROCEDURES
1.1 Aerodrome Traffic Pattern
Traffic pattern shown in diagram. The unlighted 896 feet hill, 2.2 NM NNE from runway 18 constitutes a hazard to VFR operation:
1.1.1 Rectangular
Altitude 1 400 ft MSL
Left traffic for runway 18
Right traffic for runway 36
Enter downwind leg at a 45 Degree
1.1.2 Overhead approach
Altitude 1 900 ft MSL
Runway 18 left turns
Runway 36 right turns
Enter final at a 45 degree.
1.1.3 Helicopter
Altitude 800 ft MSL
Right traffic for runway 18
Left traffic for runway 36
Landing on taxiway E
Enter downwind leg at a 45 Degree
VFR TRAFFIC PATTERNS FIELD ELEVATION 59 FT
1.2 HELICOPTER OPERATIONS
1.2.1 Departure
Normally, ATC may issue take off clearance on Taxiway E. If traffic not permit Pilots-in-command of Departing helicopters will be issued take off clearance on the runway.
After taking off helicopter shall continue climbing straight ahead until passing the departure end of The runway or Taxiway E Unless safety dictates otherwise or when specifically authorized by the tower.
1.2.2 Arrival
Pilots-in-command of VFR arriving helicopters or IFR wishing to make an Visual Approach for landing will be issued landing clearance on Taxiway E.
Pilots-in-command of arriving helicopters wishing to make an Instrument Approach for landing will be issued landing clearance on The runway.
For traffic management or Pilot-in-command of arriving helicopters request for landing position ATC may issue Landing clearance on the runway or taxiway E depend on traffics situation.
2. AIRCRAFT START UP AND / OR PUSH BACK
2.1 The pilot-in-command of a departing aircraft shall contact U-TAPAO GROUND ON FRQ. 121.9 MHz for ATC clearance 5 minutes before engine start.
2.2 All aircraft shall obtain approval from air traffic control for engine start and/or push back.
2.3 The pilot in-command and aircraft marshaller shall be responsible for the safety of the aircraft with respect to all other aircraft, vehicles, persons and other obstruction during engine start-up power-out and taxiing, and also ensuring appropriate blast zones are clear during engine start-up.
3. RADIO COMMUNICATIONS FAILURE
3.1 Departing Aircraft
3.1.1 Aircraft shall not be permitted to take-off unless two-way radio communication can be maintained with the control tower.
3.1.2 If an aircraft experiences radio communications failure after departure, the pilot shall comply with the VFR cruising altitude.
3.2 Arriving Aircraft
3.2.1 When aircraft radio receiver inoperative, report their position, distance, heading, altitude and departure point when approaching 5O NM from U-Tapao Rayong Pattaya International Airport by transmitting in the blind.
3.2.2 When two-way communications failure, radio transmitter or receiver inoperative, observe the direction of traffic in the pattern, and enter downwind with the flow of traffic.
3.2.2.1 Day time
Joining the traffic pattern of the landing runway be conformed to the altitude for the type of aircraft as listed in item 1, then make a low approach along the runway at 500 ft above the terrain, rocking wings of the aircraft until it reaches end of the runway.
Re-enter downwind leg and observe light signals from the control tower for light signal on base leg and final approach.
3.2.2.2 Night time
Joining the traffic pattern of the landing runway be conformed to the altitude for the type of aircraft as listed in item 1, then make a low approach along the runway at 500 ft above the terrain, and blinking the landing light until it reaches end of runway.
Re-enter downwind leg and observe light signals from the control tower for light signal on base leg and final approach.
4. LOCAL FLYING RESTRICTION
Flying over The gas separation plant in Rayong (Ban Map Ta Phut) is hazardous. Aircraft are to avoid areas starting from 12 41 12.00 N 101 08 00.00 E then clockwise along an arc of 2 NM radius from 12 43 00.00 N 101 09 00.00 E to 12 43 00.00 N 101 11 00.00 E then direct to 12 38 30.00 N 101 11 00.00 E from this point make an arc of 5 NM radius from 12 43 00.00 N 101 09 00.00 E clockwise to 12 38 12.00 N 101 08 00.00 E then direct to the starting point, altitude 2 000 ft.

AD 2.22 FLIGHT PROCEDURES
1. GENERAL PROCEDURES
1.1 All aircraft flying within U-TAPAO Controlled airspaces shall be provided Approach Control procedural Service.
1.2 All Aircraft shall obtain appropriate ATC Clearance before penetrating the affected airspace.
1.3 Except when necessary for take-off or landing, or except when specifically authorized by the U-tapao Approach Control, an IFR flight shall be flown at a level which is not below the Minimum Altitude for the aircraft operating IFR flight within BUT TMA or, minimum flight altitude has been established, descend below this level is only permitted in accordance with published Instrument Approach Procedures or ATS Routes Structure or Minimum Sector Altitude or when the aircraft having reported the terrain in-sight and has been cleared for the visual approach.
1.4 Transition level is FL 130 and transition altitude is altitude 11 000 ft.
2. OPERATING PROCEDURES AND ESTABLISHMENT OF STANDARD DEPARTURE CHART – INSTRUMENT (SID)
In order to enhance ATS efficiency, Air Traffic safety and avoiding congested area in Bangkok Terminal Control Area, operational implementation to enhance Air Traffic Flow Management and Safety for aircraft intending to depart at U-TAPAO Rayong Pattaya International airport (VTBU) via R201 will be applied as follows:

AD 2.23 ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
1. BIRD CONCENTRATIONS IN THE VICINITY OF U-TAPAO RAYONG PATTAYA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT
It has been observed that migratory birds appear on or in the vicinity of U-Tapao Rayong Pattaya International Airport mostly depends on the time of year and geographical conditions which may divided into Rainy season (May to October), Winter season (October to February) and Summer season (March to April), while the resident or presumed resident birds are present in variable number every month.
