AD 2.23 ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
1. Bird concentrations in the vicinity of Prachuap Khiri Khan Aerodrome
1.1 It has been observed that migratory and resident birds in remarkable numbers appear on or in the vicinity of Prachuap Khiri Khan Aerodrome and they are present in variable numbers every month. Mostly all year. Pilots are essentially requested to report bird strikes to the Aerodrome Control Tower via:
Prachuap Khiri Khan Aerodrome Control Tower
Phone +663 260 3919Bird Species that has potential hazard to aircraft are as follows:
Species | Weight (kg) | Period
Asian Openbill | 1.30 - 2.30 | All year (mostly in June, July, August)
Cattle Egret | 0.27 - 0.51 | All year
Pigeon | 0.24 - 0.38 | All year
Barn Swallow | 0.16 – 0.22 | All year
Hawk | 0.70 – 1.50 | In November to March
