AD 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS
The Royal Thai Air Force notifies that Kamphaeng Saen Air Force Base (Kamphaeng Saen AFB) air traffic services (ATS) are basically available for military aircraft and government owned aircraft. Therefore, the Kamphaeng Saen AFB is not allowed to use as an alternate aerodrome for civilian aircraft.
