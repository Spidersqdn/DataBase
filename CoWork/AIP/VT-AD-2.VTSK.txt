AD 2.20 LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS
1. AERODROME REGULATIONS
The Royal Thai Air Force notifies that Air Task Force 9 (ATF9) air traffic services (ATS) are basically available for military aircraft and government owned aircraft. Therefore, the Air Task Force 9 (ATF9) is not allowed to use as an alternate aerodrome for civilian aircraft.
2. LINE UP PROCEDURE
2.1 Using RWY 08
Aircraft taxiing out from TWY A can line up on RWY 08 directly.
Aircraft taxiing out from TWY B shall turn 180 degrees at the RWY turn pad before lining up on RWY 08.
2.2 Using RWY 26
Aircraft taxiing out from TWY A and TWY B shall turn 180 degrees at the RWY turn pad before lining up on RWY 26.
3. RUNWAY VACATING PROCEDURE
Aircraft landing on RWY 08 shall backtrack along the runway before vacating.
Aircraft landing on RWY 26 may vacate the runway at the exit point.

AD 2.22 FLIGHT PROCEDURES
1. AERODROME TRAFFIC PATTERN
1.1 Rectangular traffic pattern
Jet, Turboprop ALT 1500 ft.
Conventional ALT 1000 ft.
Light aircraft ALT 800 ft.
Helicopter ALT 500 ft.
Using both sides of traffic circuit.
1.2 Overhead approach pattern
Using runway 08 by right turn pattern.
Using runway 26 by left turn pattern.
2. VFR HELICOPTER FLIGHT PROCEDURES
2.1 Helicopters departure or arrival at Ingkhayutthaborihan Camp
For Helicopters landing at Ingkhayutthaborihan Camp only, final runway 26 is designated as " Final, Ingkhayutthaborihan Camp ".
2.1.1 Departure Procedure
The pilots shall request takeoff clearance from Pattani Tower After receiving take off clearance and becoming airborne:
Fly pass RWY 08 until crossing the departure end of runway
Then execute either:
A left turn if outbound to Hat Yai or Songkhla
A right turn if outbound to Narathiwat or Yala
2.1.2 Arrival Procedure
The pilots shall request landing clearance from Pattani Tower.After receiving landing clearance:
For helicopters inbound from Hat Yai or Songkhla:
Join right downwind RWY 26 When established on final RWY 26, the pilots shall report: " Final, Ingkhayutthaborihan Camp "
For helicopters inbound from Narathiwat or Yala:
Join final RWY 26 directly When established on final RWY 26, the pilots shall report: " Final, Ingkhayutthaborihan Camp "
All arriving helicopters shall:
Fly pass RWY 26 until crossing the departure end of runway,Then proceed to land at Ingkhayutthaborihan Camp
2.2 Helicopters departure and arrival at 15th infantry regiment
Helicopters shall use the active runway for takeoff and landing unless otherwise approved by ATC.
2.2.1 Departure Procedure
Pilots shall request take off clearance from Pattani tower.After that helicopter shall air taxi to active runway as instructed by ATC.
2.2.2 Arrival Procedure
Pilots shall request landing clearance from Pattani tower.After landing Helicopters shall air taxi to 15th infantry regiment parking as instructed by ATC.
2.3 Unless required for flight safety or when specifically requested by the pilot, ATC may issue alternate instructions.

AD 2.23 ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
1. BIRD CONCENTRATIONS
1.1 Bird concentrations in the vicinity of the aerodrome. Resident birds and migratory birds are commonly found at Pattani Airport.
1.2 There are many birds throughout the year, mostly during the rainy season (May to December). Birds may use the aerodrome as their feeding and nesting ground.
1.3 There could be some activities to reduce bird populations, such as mowing the grass and plants. Grass mowing takes place in various areas such as the runway strip and taxiway strip. This activity may attract birds from sunrise to sunset. Pilots are advised to exercise caution.
1.4 Flocks of birds often gather on the runway at night, creating a hazard for aircraft.
2. KITE FLYING NEAR AERODROME
The kite-flying season in the southern region occurs during the summer(January to May). This activity typically takes place within 1 nautical mile (NM) of an aerodrome, primarily in the northern, eastern, and southern areas around it. The kites do not exceed an altitude of 1,000 feet AGL and are flown daily between 4:00 PM and 8:00 PM.
